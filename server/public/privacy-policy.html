QuickBite — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

Welcome to QuickBite, the smart campus canteen dining and food pre-order platform. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us.

1. Information We Collect

To provide digital token generation, live kitchen updates, and food pre-ordering, we collect:

  • Account Details: Student Name, College Email address, Student Registration Number, Department/Course, and Phone Number.
  • Dining & Order Information: Selected canteen, dishes ordered, pickup time preferences, payment mode, and order token numbers.
  • Device & Notification Data: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens solely to deliver real-time order status notifications.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, verify, and fulfill canteen food orders.
  • To send real-time preparation status and token ready-for-pickup notifications.
  • To verify student identity and prevent fraudulent counter claims.
  • To securely authenticate your account and facilitate password recovery via one-time verification codes (OTPs).

3. Data Protection and Security

All network communications between the QuickBite app and our servers are encrypted using Industry Standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Passwords and OTPs are cryptographically hashed and never stored or visible in plain text. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any third parties or advertisers.

4. User Rights and Data Deletion

You have full control over your data. You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time either within the QuickBite app settings or by visiting our Account Deletion Request Page.
