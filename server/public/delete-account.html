QuickBite — Request Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policy, QuickBite provides students and canteen staff with complete control over their accounts and data.

How to delete your account:

  1. In-App: Open the QuickBite app > navigate to the Profile tab > scroll to Help & Support > tap Delete Account.
  2. Online / Email: Send an email from your registered college email address to quickbites.connecting@gmail.com with the subject Delete QuickBite Account and your Student Registration Number.

What happens when your account is deleted:

  • Your personal credentials (name, email, password hashes, phone number, and registration number) are permanently removed from our active database.
  • Active device notification tokens (FCM) are disassociated and removed immediately.
  • Past fulfilled orders are anonymized for cafeteria accounting records.
Direct Support Contact:
Email: quickbites.connecting@gmail.com
Response Time: Within 24-48 hours.
